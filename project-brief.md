# My Project brief
# Health Facility Accessibility in Ibadan North
## The question
Which settlements in Ibadan North local government area have limited access to health facilities?
## Why it matters
Understanding which settlements have limited access to health facilities can help urban planners and healthcare stakeholders identify areas that may be underserved. The findings could support decisions about where additional health facilities or improved connections to existing facilities may be needed.
## The data I need
- Health facility locations and attributes-- GRID3: https://data.humdata.org/m/dataset/grid3-nga-health-facilities-v3-0- GPKG:33.51MB
- Settlement extents-- GRID3: https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-settlement-extents-v4-1/about- GPKG: 70.8MB
- Ward boundaries-- GRID3: https://data.humdata.org/m/dataset/grid3-nga-operational-wards-v3-0- GPKG: 199.29MB
- Population distribution: https://data.humdata.org/m/dataset/bottom-up-gridded-population-estimates-for-nigeria-ZIP: 2.68MB
- Road network-- GRID3: https://data.humdata.org/m/dataset/grid3-nga-roads-v1-0- GPKG: 825MB
- ## What I would build
A map-based planning output showing health facility locations and the accessibility of healthcare services across settlements in Ibadan North. The output would highlight settlements with potentially limited access to health facilities and provide spatial evidence that can support healthcare and urban planning decisions.
